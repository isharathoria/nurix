Architecture diagram

⌄

Bottom line: automation stops exactly where a lease commitment begins. Anything cheap to undo and low-stakes is automated end to end; anything expensive to undo, or outside the model's actual evidence, stays a human call.

✅ What gets automated
  • Ingest & normalize the C&W export and Samsung's 9-store history
  • Apply hard filters (GLA, rent ceiling, cannibalization radius) before scoring, not inferred
  • Score and rank candidate malls with a confidence flag per site
  • Answer conversational queries grounded in the data, read-only
🧑‍⚖️ Stays human
  • Final site-selection decision and LOI sign-off
  • Any lease or financial commitment
  • Overriding or reweighting the model's ranking

Where does each step actually run?

⌄
Integrations needed for this POC:
  • Batch ingestion (CSV/Excel or scheduled API pull) — C&W's export + Samsung's BI/store-performance system, inbound only
  • Dashboard delivery — ranked shortlist + confidence flags surfaced to the RE committee, read-only
  • No write-back — nothing writes into Samsung's lease management or CRM systems of record during the POC; revisit integration depth after validation

What do we still need to discover?

⌄

Prioritized — each answer materially changes the design. Avoiding a generic questionnaire: these are the questions that follow directly from this specific opportunity, with the reasoning behind each one made explicit rather than left implied.

P0
Shapes the core design
  • How does Samsung define a “successful” store — sales/sq ft target, breakeven timeline, brand goals?
  • Is the 70–80 store rollout phased by region or on one fixed timeline?
  • Does C&W provide broker notes/insights alongside structured fields, or only structured data?
  • Can C&W's data be shared with a third party — and in what format?
P1
Shapes data & integration scope
  • What format is the C&W dataset in, and how complete is it across all ~350 malls?
  • What historical detail exists for the 9 current stores, and in which system?
  • Is there comparable data from other markets to strengthen a thin US sample?
  • Is a GIS or site-selection tool already used internally that this should complement?
P2
Shapes governance & success criteria
  • Are there hard constraints — minimum GLA, exclusion radius, mall tier?
  • Does Samsung's security policy require this to run inside Samsung's own cloud tenant, or is a vendor-hosted, single-tenant environment acceptable?
  • Who signs off on POC success, and what earns a yes to production?
  • What budget and timeline exist relative to the rollout already underway?

What should we watch for?

⌄

For the most significant risks — how we'd validate or de-risk them during discovery or the POC.

🗄️
Data
Risk: Only 9 stores of ground-truth history — thin sample.
Mitigation: Explainable weighted heuristic, not a trained model; validate weights with RE team judgment.
🖥️
Technical
Risk: C&W data arrives as manual exports — coverage may be inconsistent.
Mitigation: Sample 15–20 malls from the export during discovery to check completeness first.
💼
Operational
Risk: C&W is both data source and advisor — possible channel conflict.
Mitigation: Frame NuPlay as complementary orchestration, not a replacement; confirm data-use rights in writing.
👥
Adoption
Risk: Committee may not trust a model-driven ranking for 10-year leases.
Mitigation: Keep a human sign-off gate; run alongside the existing process first; show reasoning per score.
🔗
Dependency
Risk: POC timeline may not match the pace of the rollout underway.
Mitigation: Confirm rollout cadence & regional priority so POC scope matches near-term decisions.

Scope, build plan & assumptions⌄
ScopeScore & rank ~350 candidate malls (or one priority region first) using C&W's export + Samsung's 9-store history. Deliver a ranked, explainable shortlist and a conversational query view.
What NuPlay would build
  • Data ingestion & normalization pipeline
  • Look-alike scoring model, calibrated on 9-store baseline
  • Conversational query agent + simple reviewer dashboard
What Samsung would provide
  • Access to the C&W export + confirmation it can be shared
  • The 9-store historical performance dataset
  • A defined RE contact and agreed success criteria up front
Path to production — crawl, walk, run
  • Crawl: scores run in shadow mode beside the committee's process — no decisions change yet.
  • Walk: once agreement clears the bar, the ranked list leads the committee's review.
  • Run: durable C&W feed, scores validated on real outcomes, full governance sign-off.

Success measures

  • Time from raw C&W export to a prioritized shortlist (days, not weeks)
  • Agreement rate between model ranking & committee's own picks
  • Reduction in manual analyst hours per selection cycle
  • Data coverage — % of candidate malls scoreable
  • RE team's usability / trust rating after the pilot
How to read this. Hours saved = candidate malls × hours per mall × automation rate; cost avoided = hours saved × hourly analyst cost, benchmarked against reaching full, defensible coverage by hand. Automation rate and cycle time are levers to agree on with Samsung during discovery, calibrated against the real C&W data format and the committee's own review pace, not promises made in this room; committee sign-off (Step 4) stays 100% human.
